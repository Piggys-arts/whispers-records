How we work

From a feeling
to the final master.

Whispers is built around process: listening closely, testing ideas and giving each record the attention it deserves. The studio is where the collective meets, where artists grow, and where the detail gets finished.

DJy Ma.V leads the technical work across production and mastering, while the wider team brings songwriting, vocals, direction and energy to every session. It is a warm working space: a place to try ideas, laugh, learn and turn a shared feeling into a record.
